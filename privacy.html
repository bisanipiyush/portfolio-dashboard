Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This is a private, personal tool used by a single household to track its own equity portfolios. It is not offered to the public, and no one outside the household is meant to use it.

What data it accesses

With the account owner's explicit consent through Google Sign-In, the tool reads:

  • broker contract notes and depository statements in the owner's Gmail (read-only);
  • the owner's own Google Sheets that hold the portfolio records.

How the data is used

The data is used only to record the owner's own trades and to show holdings and capital gains to the owner. It is not sold or shared, and it is not used for advertising or any other purpose.

Where the data is stored

Portfolio records are stored only in the owner's own Google Sheets. This website stores no portfolio data: pages read the owner's Sheets in the browser after sign-in, and the sign-in token is kept in memory only, never on a server.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
